# Wavemesh Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Wavemesh lets nearby phones message each other directly, without the internet. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

## The short version

- Wavemesh has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.
- We (the developer) never receive your messages, your location, or anything else you do in the app.
- Your messages and settings are stored only on your phone.

## What other phones nearby can see

To find each other, phones running Wavemesh broadcast a small amount of information to other Wavemesh users within radio range (typically up to about 70 metres):

- the display name you choose
- your device name
- a random identifier created when you install the app (it is not linked to your Apple Account, phone number, or device hardware)
- your subscription tier (free or paid)
- how your phone is connected (for example Wi-Fi or Bluetooth)
- public cryptographic keys used to verify that messages really come from you and to encrypt messages to you

## Messages

- Messages are sent from phone to phone over an encrypted connection.
- When your phone knows the recipient's key (normally exchanged automatically when you connect), messages are also end-to-end encrypted, so only the recipient can read them. These messages may be passed along through other Wavemesh users' phones to reach someone out of direct range. Those phones cannot read the content, but can see that a message is travelling, its size, and when it was sent.
- If the recipient's key is not yet known, the message is sent only directly to the recipient's phone and is never passed through anyone else.
- Message history is stored only on your phone. It is protected by your device passcode and excluded from iCloud and computer backups.

## Location

- Wavemesh uses your location only to show the direction and distance between you and a person you are connected with.
- Your location is sent only to the one person you choose, and only when you tap **Share My Location**. It is never broadcast to everyone nearby and never sent to us.
- On supported iPhones, Wavemesh can use Ultra Wideband (Nearby Interaction) for precise direction finding. This exchanges a short-lived token directly with the other phone; distance readings stay on your device.

## Notifications

Message notifications are created on your phone. Wavemesh does not use a push notification server.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

## Keeping and deleting your data

- To delete a conversation, use **Clear History** in the chat or swipe on a person in the device list.
- Deleting the app deletes your messages and settings. Your app's cryptographic identity may remain in your phone's secure keychain until you erase the device.
- Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete or send you on request.

## Children

Wavemesh is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app and at the same web address, with a new "Last updated" date.
